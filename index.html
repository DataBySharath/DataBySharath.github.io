From classroom to clean dashboards

I’m Sharath Pujar, a Consulting Analyst at Twimbit with over three years in data analytics and BI consulting. I build executive-ready Power BI dashboards and data models for global clients in ICT, financial services and infrastructure.

My path started with a deep curiosity about the stories numbers tell. After an M.E. in Information Sciences from Manipal, I worked in medical imaging and taught as an assistant professor before moving into analytics full time.

I like turning messy requirements into models that stay accurate, reports that refresh on their own, and visuals a CXO can read in ten seconds.

Off the clock I play chess, follow technology trends and tinker with productivity systems.
