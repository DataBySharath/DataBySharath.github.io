I’m Sharath Pujar, a Data Analytics Consultant with over three years of experience across banking, ICT and infrastructure. I build scalable Power BI and Microsoft Fabric solutions and deliver insights to CXO stakeholders in the US and Middle East.

My path started with a deep curiosity about the stories numbers tell. After an M.E. in Information Sciences from Manipal (9.1 CGPA), I worked on medical imaging and taught as an assistant professor before moving into analytics full time.
